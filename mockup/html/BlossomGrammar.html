Use subject + verb clauses

A1

A subject + verb clause is the simplest complete sentence in English. It tells us who or what (the subject) and what they do (the verb). Some verbs need nothing after them: She smiled. The bus arrived.

Câu đơn giản nhất = Ai / Cái gì + làm gì. Tiếng Anh luôn cần đủ cả hai phần.

Use whenyou make simple statements about actions and events. · Dùng khi kể về hành động, sự việc đơn giản.

Structure · Cấu trúc

Subject + Verb
The subject is usually a noun or pronoun. The verb tells us the action or state.
He / She / It + Verb-s / -es
In the present simple, add -s or -es after he, she, it and singular nouns. — Ngôi thứ ba số ít thêm -s/-es.
Subject + Verb + (place / time)
You can add where or when — but the sentence is already complete without it.

Examples· Ví dụ

1.
I run every morning.Subject “I” + verb “run” — a daily habit. · Tôi chạy bộ mỗi sáng.
2.
The baby cried.Subject “The baby” + verb “cried” — a finished action; nothing else is needed. · Em bé đã khóc.
3.
She works at a bakery.He / she / it → “works” with -s. · Cô ấy làm việc ở tiệm bánh.
4.
Prices went up last year.Subject “Prices” + verb “went up”; “last year” only adds time. · Giá cả đã tăng vào năm ngoái.
5.
We live near the river.Subject “We” + verb “live”; “near the river” adds place. · Chúng tôi sống gần sông.

Common mistakes· Lỗi thường gặp

She gogoes to school by bus.
He / she / it needs -s!
Ngôi thứ 3 số ít: thêm -s / -es.
RainsIt rains a lot in Huế in October.
English always needs a subject — even for weather!
Không bỏ chủ ngữ: dùng “It”.
The children are very happy today.
Adjectives aren't verbs — add be.
Tính từ cần “to be” đi kèm.
